# Notes for Claude

## Working rules
- Never commit to `main`. Work on a branch and open a PR; Zander reviews and merges (often from his phone).
- Keep PRs small and focused. Explain changes in plain language in the PR body, with a short "how to test" list.
- No secrets, API keys or `.env` files. This is a public repo and a static site.
- Keep dependencies minimal (Phaser + Vite). Ask before adding new packages.
- Run `npm run build` before pushing; test in a headless browser with `?debug` when touching movement or layout.

## Art pipeline
- Art comes from Gemini on a flat magenta (#FF00FF) background; Claude cuts it out, snaps pixel grids and scales it.
- Each build writes `version.json`; `src/systems/updateCheck.js` reloads players into newer deploys (GitHub Pages caches pages ~10 min).
- Art lives in `src/assets/` and is loaded with `asset('folder/file.png')` so builds hash file names (no stale browser caches).
- Player sheet `src/assets/player/zander.png`: 32x52 frames, 4 columns, drawn at 2x.
  Rows: 0 idle (down, left, right, up), 1-4 walk down/left/right/up, 5-8 carry down/left/right/up, 9 work (back view).
- Augustus sheet `src/assets/augustus/augustus.png`: 20x26 frames drawn at 3x: 0 front, 1 side (faces right; flip for left), 2 drowsy (eyes off), 3-5 sleeping on his cloud. Cut from Zander's Gemini sheets, which use a 60-pixel-wide art grid (1376/60 = 22.93 px per art pixel).
- Nero sheet `src/assets/nero/nero.png`: 28x30 frames drawn at 3x: 0 front, 1 side (faces right), 2 rogue front, 3 rogue side. His Gemini sheet draws him at half that pixel size, so he's snapped on a 120-wide grid (11.47 px) to keep his face detail; his cloud is built from the strip's rounded end, a middle slice and the mirrored end.
- Rooms are 1376x768 isometric-style images; world coordinates match image pixels.
- Stations are separate sprites anchored at bottom-center with a floor `footprint` polygon (see `src/data/dorm.js`).

## Game direction (agreed so far)
- Money: hustles (active tasks at stations), bug bounties, idle stations running AI agent businesses.
- Defense events pop up; unfixed systems go down for a while.
- Real cybersecurity terms and tools.
- Hires: AI agents and pen testers. Agent types live in `src/data/agents.js` (`AGENT_TYPES`; up to `MAX_AGENTS` = 2 in the dorm, any mix (it was 3: older saves send the last-hired extra home on load and refund its hire price, `agentsSentHome`, banner); each extra of a type costs double; agents of a type share `agentLevels[type]`; names Augustus, Augustus II). One class drives them all: `src/systems/Agent.js` (reads its type config). Augustus (numbers in `src/data/augustus.js`): hired in the shop's Agents tab, rides his cloud, works the best-paying usable machine at `workRate` (stations get `rate`/`bonus`/`helper` in `hustle.update`), takes piles to the hamper, naps 60 s when energy runs out. He never resets the breaker, repairs, carries boxes or shops. Skips fried/overheated/full/unpowered machines and the one the player is at. The scene keeps `this.agents` and `this.arrivals` (hired, floating in); saves store `agents`, `arrivals`, `agentLevels` (old single-`augustus` saves are converted on load). Machines an agent works show a compact panel (bars only). Upgrades: speed, profit, energy (5 levels, price doubles).
- Nero (`src/data/nero.js`): $2,500, 0.8x your job speed, +50% pay, never naps. Types with a `rogue` config go rogue every `rogueEvery` seconds (240-360, stretched by the stability upgrade): state 'rogue', races to random machines and fries each after 2 s (never in the first 5 s after loading). The player contains him by staying within `catchRange` for `catchSeconds` (progress decays when apart); then 'quarantined' for 60 s and back to work. Saved per agent: `rogue`, `rogueIn`, `quarantine`. Debug keys N (hire Nero) and X (Nero goes rogue).
- Currency is cash only; cash counts once stashed in the laundry basket by the bed.
- Neighbor (`src/systems/Neighbor.js`, numbers in `src/data/neighbor.js`): machines being worked make `noise` (catalog.js; rack 4; the starter desk is silent). The total maps to a `NOISE_LEVELS` entry (Low 1+, Medium 3+, High 6+) whose `visitEvery` (300/180/120 s) sets how fast annoyance fills; silence slowly drains it (`calmPerSecond`). At 50 they bang on the wall, at 100 they come over (10 s countdown, `comingSeconds`). He appears in the right-wall window: `window-open.png` (the room's window with blinds up and the glass cut out) is drawn over him and `window-sky.png` behind, so the sill hides his legs. Sheet `src/assets/neighbor/neighbor.png`: 38x52 frames at 2x: 0 knocking (faces left), 1 arms crossed, 2 pointing, 3 walking away. During the countdown and visit (`neighbor.quiet`), walking up to a machine switches it off (`hustle.switchedOff`, gray tint, agents skip it); any other time, walking up to an off machine switches it on. Anything on, powered and not fried when the countdown ends = a fine (`gameState.charge`, $50 doubling to $800), the pointing pose and a longer stay; quiet = arms crossed. Annoyance starts over after each visit. Saved as `neighbor` + `off` per machine; a visit in progress restarts its countdown on load. Debug key L.
- Foam panels (`FOAM_LEVELS`/`FOAM_ITEMS` in catalog.js; art `src/assets/foam/`, Zander's Gemini tiles snapped on the 240-wide grid, 50x51, drawn at 1x): 3 levels ($150/$300/$600), bought one at a time like power (`nextFoam()`, `foamPending()`). The box goes to `DORM.foamWall` (side of the bed); `installFoam()` raises `scene.foamLevel` and `drawFoam()` tiles the back wall above the bed (2x2, 3x2, 3x3, charcoal/navy checkerboard, depth -9500). Each level multiplies the neighbor's patience drain by 1 - `FOAM_QUIET_PER_LEVEL` (0.1). Saved as `foamLevel`. Debug key F: next level now.
- Clock (`src/systems/Clock.js`, numbers in `src/data/clock.js`): game time, a full day every 600 s of play (6 AM-10 PM day, 10 PM-6 AM night = quiet hours). `clock.night`, `clock.darkness` (0..1 with an hour of dusk/dawn fade) drives `nightShade` (navy overlay at depth 10990, under the power-out darkness and all labels). Emits 'night-start'/'night-end' (banners). At night the neighbor's patience drains `nightMultiplier` (2) times faster and his window shows `window-sky-night.png`. HUD clock panel next to the cash. Saved as `clock` {day, minutes}; restored before the neighbor. Debug key T: +2 hours.
- Player upgrades (`src/data/playerUpgrades.js`, the shop's You tab): carry limit ($100 → $1000; `collectFrom` takes only what fits and leaves the rest on the machine; digging a machine up with the shovel takes all of it), speed (+10%/level via `player.speedMultiplier`; movement is sub-stepped so fast walking can't clip furniture) and work speed (+20%/level, `scene.workRate` passed as `rate` to the station you work; up to 2x). You should always out-work agents: Augustus `workRate` 0.5 (+12%/speed level, max 0.8), Nero 0.8 (max ~1.3, +50% pay). Saved as `playerLevels`.
- The dorm starts empty. The corner desk is the free starter workstation and the shop screen.
- Items live in `src/data/catalog.js` (price, watts, delivery time, footprint). Install with `placeItem(itemId, plotId)`, remove with `removeItem(plotId)`, then call `rebuildNavigation()`.
- Limits are POWER (upgradable power supply, meter in the HUD; over capacity trips the breaker) and HEAT (machines heat up with use and need a cooldown; fans attach to a machine to cool it).
- Power: `POWER_TIERS`/`POWER_ITEMS` in catalog.js. The shop offers only the next tier, one order at a time; the box goes to the breaker panel (`DORM.breaker`). The starter laptop draws `STARTER_LAPTOP_WATTS` (60). Over capacity for 1.2 s (lights flicker) trips the breaker (`gameState.tripped`): machines get `hustle.powered = false`, the room dims and flickers, the shop laptop stays on battery. Each trip fries 1 random machine (2 on a re-trip right after a reset; none in the first 5 s after loading): `hustle.broken`, smoke + dark tint, repaired by standing at it 3 s with power on. Light flicker patterns stay at 2 flashes per second or fewer (photosensitivity). Reset = stand still at the panel 1.5 s; if still overloaded it re-trips and you must step away before retrying. Installing power at the panel also resets it.
- Shop: the corner laptop does hustles until stashed cash reaches $50 (`SHOP_UNLOCK_CASH`), then becomes the shop (`src/ui/ShopPanel.js`). Safety net: if broke with no earning machine, the laptop does hustles again.
- Buying: order at the shop, box appears at the door after the item's delivery time, carry it onto an empty plot to install. A machine box isn't picked up while there's no empty plot (hint: dig one up); walking back to the door with a box sets it down there (`setBoxDown`; `leftDoor` makes you step `DOOR_LEAVE` away first so pick-up and drop-off don't bounce). The shovel doesn't work with a box in your hands. Each item's `earns` config (catalog.js) creates a HustleStation: 'work' (stand at it) or 'idle' (runs alone; unused now, the server rack is a work machine). Shovel: HUD button when standing at an installed item, tap twice; `shovel(plotId)` removes it and puts 25% of the price (`SHOVEL_REFUND`) in your hands as cash. Use `orderable(id)` for anything that can be ordered (machines and power).
- Hustles: `src/data/hustles.js` (jobs, heat, pile cap) and `src/systems/HustleStation.js`. Standing still at a station works it; pay goes to a desk pile; leaving picks the pile up; the stash spot banks it.
- Security questions: `src/data/questions.js` (original, Zander-reviewed bank: helpdesk / web / ai, matched to the machine's jobs). When the player (never a helper) finishes a job, 1 in 5 (`QUESTION_CHANCE`) calls `DormScene.askQuestion`; the job waits (`hustle.awaiting`) for `answerQuestion`: right = double pay, wrong or 15 s timeout = job fails. No repeats until a group's bank is used up (`questionsSeen`, saved). Card UI: `src/ui/QuestionPanel.js`. Debug key Q: a question on every job.
- Waking agents: after the 60 s nap an agent stays asleep until the player walks within `WAKE_RANGE` (80) of him (`agent.waitingToWake`, label + chip + roster line; saved per agent as `asleep`), so the dorm can't earn unattended. Nero never naps.
- Saving: `src/state/save.js` (localStorage, versioned, validated on load). When you add game state, add it to `saveProgress()`/`restoreProgress()` in DormScene and to `readSave()` checks; bump VERSION if the shape changes.
- Maintenance (`src/data/maintenance.js`, logic in HustleStation): servers (`SERVER_JOBS`: rack, GPU rig) can crash in `finishJob` (4% + up to 8% more at full heat, job lost): `setBroken(true, { kind: 'crashed' })`, blue tint, no smoke, `hustle.crashed`; reboot = stand 3 s (same path as fried). PCs (`PC_JOBS`: laptops, workstation) get `updateDue` every 150-270 s of powered time; you standing there installs it first (3 s, `updateInstall`, no job progress); after 60 s unattended it's `outdated` (pay x0.75). Helpers never reboot or install. `scene.crashedStations` / `scene.pendingUpdates` drive the chips. Saved per machine: `crashed`, `updateIn`, `updateDue`, `updateAge`. Debug keys C (crash a server) and V (updates on every PC). Energy drinks give a speed boost (not built). The player should always be busy.
- Prestige comes later. Finish the dorm section first.
